Privacy

A privacy project's website should have a short privacy page. This is it.

This site collects nothing. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no cookies, no fingerprinting, and no account system. The only JavaScript on the site is a local scroll-reveal animation that runs in your browser and never transmits anything.
Requests

Everything loads from this site

Fonts are self-hosted, so the pages make no third-party requests. What your browser fetches is limited to the static files of this site, and standard web-server access logs may apply on the hosting layer.

Outbound links

External sites are their own policies

Links to GitHub and other services leave this site; those services handle your data under their own terms.

The protocol

Site privacy is not protocol privacy

Coppice Names operations are public Zcash transactions by design. What the protocol hides — spending authority, commit openings, note plaintexts — and what it deliberately publishes are analyzed in the whitepaper's privacy section.

Changes

Kept honest in the open

This site is a static repository: any change to what it serves is visible in its version history. If analytics or cookies are ever added, this page will change first.

Questions or concerns? Open an issue on the site repository or the protocol repository.
